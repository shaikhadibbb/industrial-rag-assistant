Since January 2022, the GST Council made GSTR-2B the **mandatory basis** for ITC claims under Rule 36(4). Yet many practitioners still use GSTR-2A out of habit. Here's why that's a costly mistake. ## What is GSTR-2A? GSTR-2A is a **dynamic, auto-populated** statement that updates every time a supplier files their GSTR-1. It reflects real-time supplier filings and changes frequently. ## What is GSTR-2B? GSTR-2B is a **static, cut-off based** statement generated on the 14th of every month. It shows exactly what ITC is available for a specific tax period and does not change after generation. ## Why GSTR-2B is Now Mandatory Under Rule 36(4) of the CGST Rules, ITC can only be claimed on invoices that appear in GSTR-2B. Using GSTR-2A for reconciliation will result in: - Mismatches with the GST portal - Potential notice from GSTIN authorities - Excess ITC claims that must be reversed with interest ## How Gestly Uses GSTR-2B Gestly's reconciliation engine compares your Purchase Register exclusively against GSTR-2B. This ensures every ITC claim is compliant with the latest Rule 36(4) requirements.
